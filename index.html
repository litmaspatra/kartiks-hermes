Personal Workspace Assistant

One private assistant for Kartik’s daily work.

Kartik’s Hermes is a personal-use assistant operated by Kartik. It connects to Kartik’s Google Account only after explicit OAuth authorization and performs user-requested tasks across selected Google Workspace services.

Read the privacy policy Manage Google access ↗

Purpose

Designed for one owner, not the public.

Kartik’s Hermes is not a public software-as-a-service product and does not offer public registration. It is used by its owner to organize schedules, handle requested communications, manage personal files, and automate routine Workspace actions.

Google Workspace access

What the integration can do

Gmail

Search, read, organize, draft, and send messages when requested by the owner.

Calendar

Read schedules and create, update, or remove owner-requested events and reminders.

Drive

Find and read files, and create or manage files the owner explicitly directs the assistant to handle.

Docs & Sheets

Read and edit documents or spreadsheets to complete specific owner-requested tasks.

Contacts

Read contact details to resolve recipients for actions initiated by the owner.

Owner control

Every authorization can be reviewed or revoked from the owner’s Google Account at any time.

Control and transparency

Your data remains under your direction.

Google data is accessed only to provide requested assistant functions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or made available to unrelated third parties. See the Privacy Policy for access, processing, retention, and deletion details.
