Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Kartik’s Hermes accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data.

Effective and last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Scope and operator

Kartik’s Hermes (“the application”) is a personal-use assistant operated by Kartik for Kartik’s own Google Account. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide public account registration. Questions about this policy can be directed through the operator’s GitHub profile.

2. Google data the application accesses

Depending on the permissions granted by the owner, the application may access:

  • Gmail: message metadata, message content, labels, drafts, and sending or modifying messages.
  • Google Calendar: calendars, events, reminders, attendees, and related event metadata.
  • Google Drive: file names, metadata, file content, and files created or selected for management by the owner.
  • Google Docs and Sheets: document or spreadsheet content and edits requested by the owner.
  • Google Contacts: contact names, email addresses, and telephone numbers used to resolve owner-requested actions.
  • OAuth account information: authorization tokens and technical metadata required to maintain the authorized connection.

3. How Google data is used

The application uses Google data only to provide functions initiated or configured by the owner, including:

  • showing schedules and preparing personal briefings;
  • creating and managing calendar events or reminders;
  • searching, reading, organizing, drafting, or sending email;
  • finding, reading, creating, or updating owner-selected files, documents, and spreadsheets;
  • resolving contacts for owner-requested communications; and
  • maintaining the authenticated Google connection.

Google data is not used for advertising, marketing, creditworthiness decisions, or sale. The application operator does not use Google user data to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Processing and sharing

The application does not sell Google user data and does not disclose it to unrelated third parties. When a requested task requires AI interpretation, the minimum content reasonably necessary may be processed by the AI infrastructure configured by the owner. Such processing is performed solely to provide the requested assistant function.

Data may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect the security of the owner or application, or with the owner’s explicit direction. No other transfer is permitted.

5. Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner-controlled Hermes environment on the owner’s device. Reasonable technical safeguards, including access controls and encrypted transport to Google APIs, are used. Google data returned for a task may appear in local command output, application logs, or assistant conversation history under the owner’s control.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. The owner is responsible for securing the device, Google Account, GitHub account, and any configured AI provider accounts.

6. Retention and deletion

Google data is retained only for as long as needed to complete requested tasks, maintain owner-configured records, or support local history and troubleshooting. The owner can delete local Hermes data and generated files at any time.

The owner can stop future access by visiting Google Account third-party connections, selecting Kartik’s Hermes, and revoking access. Revocation prevents new API access but does not automatically delete local copies already created at the owner’s direction. Those copies can be removed from the owner’s device separately.

7. Google API Limited Use disclosure

Kartik’s Hermes’ use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are published.
